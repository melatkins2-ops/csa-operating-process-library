NO WRONG DOOR STANDARD

Receive → Understand → Connect → Confirm

The person who receives the request does not have to solve everything. They do make sure the request reaches the right place and the next step is clear.

Done means: the receiving owner has accepted the connection and the requester knows what happens next. Forwarding an email by itself is not completion.

Not sure which resource you need?

CSA resources can serve different purposes. You do not need to know the type before you start.

See definitions & examples
Written ProcessHow does the work move from beginning to end? Shows the starting point, major steps, who is involved, the sequence, and what happens at the end.
Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)How do we consistently carry out this work? Adds responsibilities, detailed steps, changes in the path, documentation, and a clear finish.
ProcedureHow do I complete this specific task? Gives focused, step-by-step instructions for one task or part of a larger process.
Job Aid / ToolWhat can help me do or decide something? Includes checklists, templates, forms, matrices, decision guides, intervention guides, quick references, and calculators.
System / User GuideHow do I use this system or application? Teaches staff how to navigate and use a system, often with screenshots and task instructions.
Handbook / GuideWhere can I find several related resources? Brings multiple processes, procedures, tools, guidance, FAQs, and reference information together.
How they work together: One source can contain more than one resource type. A handbook may contain processes and procedures; a system guide may include task procedures; a job aid can support a process or SOP. The Builder should preserve the source and help separate or connect resources only when that adds value.
